01 · BRAND OVERVIEW

Meet Vastra Vibe.

Traditional roots. Contemporary rhythm.

Vastra Vibe is a fictional contemporary Indian ethnic-fashion brand created for this Facebook Ads capstone project. The brand brings traditional Indian clothing into a modern, youthful lifestyle.

Its personality is modern, elegant, youthful, stylish and culturally connected.

BRAND PROMISE

Wear your culture.
Make it your own.
